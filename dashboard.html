Sam, from Sean:Your Nov 3 list, in order.
  • Rolling 12 months replaces "this year" everywhere. The date range in the filter bar applies to when a plan was started or an occurrence happened. Active, due and overdue are always as of today.
  • Every tile is a link. Click "Reviews overdue" and you land on that list, already filtered.
  • Filter bar: building (pick "All buildings" for the corporate view), department, date range with a custom from/to. Department doesn't apply to occurrences, since they don't carry one.
  • Survey files: a POC can belong to a file ("March 2026 Annual Survey"). The file counts as one item on every tile and chart; open it in the list to see the POCs inside. The by-department chart counts standalone POCs and lists the file's inside breakdown underneath.
  • Areas to be aware of: residents, staff IDs, places, shifts and types that repeat, with a "Start a POC" link that pre-fills the form. "Where did it happen?" is our own box in the tracker; the portal never asks it and it's never copied out.
  • Resident autofill is in the tracker: a resident number from an earlier occurrence fills gender, age, cognitive and physical status. No birthday or name, since we never store those.
  • Summary writes the monthly or weekly digest per building: copy it, or see the email it would send. Actually sending it comes with the real app's email service.
Buildings B and C are sample data so the corporate pieces have something to show; Heights' occurrences are whatever is in the tracker.
